# affectUI – illustrations

Every file is a single, self-contained React component (React is the only dependency).
Copy a file into your project, e.g. `components/ui/`, and import it from there.
Some files import another affectUI component from `@/components/ui/<name>` – copy that one alongside.

- **Agent Team** (`agent-team.tsx`) – A prompt types itself and branches out into three agents, each with a role and overlapping tool logos
- **Cover Flow** (`cover-flow.tsx`) – Browse covers in 3D – drag, flick or scrub the row, it springs to the nearest cover; images drift in their frames over a soft reflection
- **Empty Calendar** (`empty-calendar.tsx`) – Stack of calendar cards marked empty, fanning out on hover
- **Enrichment Orbit** (`enrichment-orbit.tsx`) – Grok tile inside two rings with comets and data nodes orbiting around it
- **Frost Cloud** (`frost-cloud.tsx`) – A frosted white cloud with eyes cut out of it, on a red-orange or blue sky – it floats, turns to look around, blinks, follows the pointer and bounces with a wink when clicked
- **Ghosted Chat** (`ghosted-chat.tsx`) – Unanswered chat messages marked as read, with a thumbs-down reply on hover
- **Glass Folder** (`glass-folder.tsx`) – Glassy blue folder with documents behind a frosted pocket that tips open on hover
- **Glow Cloud** (`glow-cloud.tsx`) – A glowing cloud with eyes in blue or red-orange – it floats, turns to look around, blinks, follows the pointer and bounces with a wink when clicked
- **Glow Keyboard** (`glow-keyboard.tsx`) – AI chat input that types itself over a glass keyboard – every key press sends a wave up the gradient floor
- **Integration Beam** (`integration-beam.tsx`) – Your product between the tools it connects – light pulses flow along curved beams, hover to focus a tool, click to pause it
- **Integration Flow** (`integration-flow.tsx`) – Integration pills rotating along a dashed arc, each beaming its data into a central analysis hub
- **Integration Grid** (`integration-grid.tsx`) – Honeycomb-like wall of integration logos around a bright centre tile with your product mark, fading out toward the edges
- **Layer Stack** (`layer-stack.tsx`) – Isometric stack of five layers with a list beside it – the chosen entry lifts its layer out in color
- **Lead Sourcing** (`lead-sourcing.tsx`) – ChatGPT tile linked to Gmail and Grok, with a shine running along the connection
- **Logo Cluster** (`logo-cluster.tsx`) – Your mark on a white disc in the middle – company logos bounce out of it and settle around it
- **Model Wheel** (`model-wheel.tsx`) – AI model names on a turning drum – the rows bend away at the edges and each model lands on a soft gray fill
- **Outreach Branches** (`outreach-branches.tsx`) – Lines branching out to Gmail, Slack and Notion with colored pulses flowing down
- **Project Flow** (`project-flow.tsx`) – Animated scene of a client task branching into subtasks, notes and assigned roles
- **DACH Region** (`region-dach.tsx`) – DACH map with fading neighbours and a pastel band along the border, switchable to the US, China, India, Brazil or Japan
- **Side Folder** (`side-folder.tsx`) – Glassy blue folder on its side, documents sliding out to the right as the pocket swings open on hover
- **Tab Overload** (`tab-overload.tsx`) – Overlapping browser windows where the highlight follows the hovered tab
- **Team Invite** (`team-invite.tsx`) – Connected teammates – a cursor drags a new one into the empty slot, the line turns solid and they go online
- **Voice Orb** (`voice-orb.tsx`) – The face of a voice assistant – a sphere of dots that breathes, catches sound, collapses into a spinning ring while thinking and ripples when it speaks
- **World Map Continental** (`world-map.tsx`) – World map with one highlighted continent and a pastel band along its coastline, switchable between all six

Docs and live previews: https://www.affectui.com
